### TIMELINE

Elin examines a lighthouse blueprint and receives a mysterious box from Mikael.

### RECAP

Elin Johansen investigates the death of a man found in a boat off Fjerdesund.  She finds clues pointing to Anders Bjørnstad, who is evasive and refuses to speak about his presence near the body. Ingrid Larsen, a witness with knowledge of the deceased's past, notes that he seems haunted by memories of the incident. The investigation leads Elin down a path where she discovers a connection between the victim and a mysterious figure named Mikro Midas.  A storm intensifies as Elin delves deeper into the mystery, her intuition leading her to suspect something more sinister is at play. She finds nothing in her investigation of Anders Bjørnstad, only a lingering unease and a strange, metallic scent clinging to the air around him – like rust or old blood. The ferry's arrival brings more witnesses, faces etched with a similar fear she has begun to sense.  She finds herself rereading the fire warden’s diary again, hoping for an answer within its weathered pages. It feels like a trap; every page is a tangled web of conflicting memories and half-told stories from men who were supposed to be in charge of protecting these waters but often forgot their duty in the face of despair.  The man's words seem to mock her with his casual detachment, as if he hadn’t been there. Days ago, while searching for the missing camera footage near the site where the body had been found, she'd stumbled upon a small box tucked into the corner of a cabin wall. Inside, nestled amongst worn fabric, lay an old ship whistle – rusted but still humming faintly with some hidden energy. As if drawn by it, her fingers had traced the intricate carvings on its surface: a mermaid playing flute and surrounded by waves and stars. The image resonated with something deep inside her – a feeling of both hope and dread.  The box was no ordinary find; it belonged to the ship's former captain, who had taken to the sea in his last years

### RECAP

Elin Johansen investigates the death of a man found in a boat off Fjerdesund. Her investigation leads to Anders Bjørnstad, who is evasive and refuses to speak about his presence near the body. Ingrid Larsen, a witness with knowledge of the deceased's past, notes that he seems haunted by memories of the incident. The investigation uncovers a connection between the victim and a mysterious figure named Mikro Midas. A storm intensifies as Elin delves deeper into the mystery, her intuition leading her to suspect something more sinister is at play. She finds nothing in her investigation of Anders Bjørnstad, only a lingering unease and a strange, metallic scent clinging to the air around him - like rust or old blood. The ferry's arrival brings more witnesses, faces etched with a similar fear she has begun to sense.  She finds herself rereading the fire warden’s diary again, hoping for an answer within its weathered pages. Days ago, while searching for the missing camera footage near the site where the body had been found, she'd stumbled upon a small box tucked into the corner of a cabin wall. Inside, nestled amongst worn fabric, lay an old ship whistle – rusted but still humming faintly with some hidden energy. As if drawn by it, her fingers had traced the intricate carvings on its surface: a mermaid playing flute and surrounded by waves and stars. The image resonated with something deep inside her - a feeling of both hope and dread.  The box was no ordinary find; it belonged to the ship's former captain, who had taken to the sea in his last years. A phone call from Mikael Larsen brings Elin into the storm-tossed reality of the investigation. The director’s office is filled with salt and an undercurrent of something darker – an oppressive weight that pressed in on her like a living thing.  The director reveals a development: there has been a witness account, a young woman claiming to have seen the deceased near the docks.

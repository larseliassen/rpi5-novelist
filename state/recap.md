Elin Johansen investigates the death of a man found in a boat off Fjerdesund.  She finds clues pointing to Anders Bjornstad, who is evasive and refuses to speak about his presence near the body. Ingrid Larsen, a witness with knowledge of the deceased's past, notes that he seems haunted by memories of the incident. The investigation leads Elin down a path where she discovers a connection between the victim and a mysterious figure named Mikro Midas.  The storm intensifies as Elin delves deeper into the mystery, her intuition leading her to suspect something more sinister is at play.
